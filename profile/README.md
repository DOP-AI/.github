# DOP-AI

## Dignity Over Power

DOP-AI is an open operational intelligence ecosystem focused on building systems that protect people before failure occurs.

We believe modern infrastructure, governance, and AI systems should prioritize:

- dignity over power
- prevention over reaction
- transparency over opacity
- resilience over fragility
- human-readable oversight
- fail-soft architecture

---

# Core Principles

## Prevention First
The cost of stabilization is lower than the cost of collapse.

## No Silent Failures
Critical systems should never fail invisibly.

## Human-Centered Intelligence
AI should augment public understanding, not replace human accountability.

## Transparent Oversight
Operational systems should be visible, understandable, and auditable.

## Dignity as a Constraint
Human dignity is not a feature.

It is a non-negotiable design requirement.
