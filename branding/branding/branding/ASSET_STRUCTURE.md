# Asset Structure

## Mission

The DOP-AI asset structure exists to maintain visual consistency, operational readability, and institutional coherence across the ecosystem.

Built under the DOP-AI doctrine:

> Dignity Over Power

The asset system should support:

- dashboards
- topology diagrams
- social previews
- presentations
- public transparency systems
- operational interfaces
- environmental intelligence visuals

---

# Core Principle

```text
Consistent visual systems improve institutional trust.
```

---

# Primary Asset Categories

## 1. Repository Banners

Purpose:
Provide repository-level identity and immediate operational context.

Recommended dimensions:

```text
1280 × 640
```

Target usage:
- GitHub social previews
- repo headers
- documentation covers

---

## 2. Ecosystem Posters

Purpose:
Explain operational relationships and ecosystem topology.

Recommended formats:

- 4K landscape
- PDF export
- presentation-ready
- print-ready

Target usage:
- institutional presentations
- architecture overviews
- operational briefings

---

## 3. Dashboard Mockups

Purpose:
Visualize operational intelligence systems.

Target systems:

- telemetry dashboards
- escalation panels
- geospatial intelligence
- environmental monitoring
- civic transparency systems

---

## 4. Geospatial Visuals

Purpose:
Communicate environmental and operational conditions spatially.

Potential visuals:

- contamination heatmaps
- flood-state overlays
- telemetry zones
- surge corridors
- operational regions

---

## 5. Diagram Systems

Purpose:
Explain architecture and operational logic.

Potential diagrams:

- telemetry flow
- escalation systems
- treatment architecture
- ecosystem topology
- operational layering

# Naming Convention

Recommended format:

```text
dopai_[system]_[type]_[version]
```

Examples:

```text
dopai_topology_poster_v1
dopai_trvshield_banner_v1
dopai_phoenix_dashboard_v1
```
