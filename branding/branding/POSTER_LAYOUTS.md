# Poster Layouts

## Mission

DOP-AI poster systems are designed to visually communicate ecosystem architecture, operational relationships, and resilience philosophy through clear institutional-grade visual layouts.

The objective is:

- rapid comprehension
- operational clarity
- visual coherence
- institutional trust
- ecosystem recognition

---

# Core Principle

```text id="yjlxro"
Visual structure improves operational understanding.
```

# Visual Style

Recommended tone:

```text id="0jlxrq"
Apple × Palantir × LCARS × Emergency Operations Center
```

Characteristics:

- dark operational background
- high contrast
- clean geometry
- minimal clutter
- topology visibility
- institutional typography

Potential elements:

# Avoid

Avoid:

- clutter
- excessive text
- meme aesthetics
- cyberpunk overload
- excessive neon
- military intimidation aesthetics

The system should visually communicate:

```text id="1jlxrr"
stability during instability.
```
